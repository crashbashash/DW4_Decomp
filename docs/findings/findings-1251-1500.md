### #1268 · confirmed

**Where:** 0x003a7260 — Near-miss shapes, wave 11

The four voice-state helpers FUN_003a7260, FUN_003a7300, FUN_003a73b0 and FUN_003a74b0 take a beCriAudio receiver: _adxt (+8), _sndf (+12), _audioType (+16; 1 and 2 are ADX voices, 3 a sound-bank voice) and _errVolume (+20).

**Notes:** FUN_003a73b0 and FUN_003a74b0 are called with handle->_audio by the matched beCri::vfunc_27, FUN_003a7300 is queried by the same loop before it steps a ramp, and all four use the same predicates (_sndf and type 3; _adxt and type 1 or 2) on the same offsets as the already-typed FUN_003a70c0 and FUN_003a6f70. The sources now derive their predicate views from beCriAudio (or take it directly) and compile byte-identical. What the helpers do is not established beyond that: they forward to ADX-driver calls (FUN_00332370, FUN_003323a0, FUN_00332430, FUN_00332460) that are not analysed here, and _errVolume holds -999 or a level, so any reading of them as pause or resume steps is a guess. The explicit-cast spelling that would let the typed-access metric count them changes the generated code, so the metric does not see these.

**Evidence:** src/unmatched/FUN_003a7260.cpp; tools/verify.py 0x3a7260
