# Dungeon floors — first dungeon (Humid Cave)

Scope: the first World-1 dungeons of the original game played natively. The Humid Cave (`dng101b1`, `dng101b2`; boss Blossomon) runs
from `stag0101`'s own exit to the dungeon's own exit; the second dungeon (`dng102b1`, `dng102b2`, `dng102b3`; boss Mammothmon) runs from `stag0101` through
`stag0102` to the Terminal. This page holds what the floors need from the port, what each piece rests on, the retail checks and
what is open. It also owns local co-op in the world and the hubs, the field HUD, loot delivery and the drop models. Field stages, the fight rules and
the Goblin Fortress chain are in [combat foundation](combat.md); the sound side is in
[audio playback](audio-playback.md#dungeon-stages-2761).

### Validation

- `make -C port check-fieldstage` runs `test_fieldstage dungeon` (about 20 s): from `stag0101` (flags 2, 355, 503) the `matz00` region sends the player to
  `dng101b1` point 0; the floor opens with 18 camera regions from its own rig, control returns, 12 generators (5 started), 10 enemy models, 3
  save points and a finite player position; its `matz02` region leads to `dng101b2` (26 regions, 16 generators, 9 started, 21 enemy models, one
  Blossomon).
- `make -C port check-dungeonroute` (about five minutes, part of `check`) plays the route for one player with the pilot bot: `dng101b1` waves
  `matg01`..`matg07` (16 kills; the floor script's door flags 101-104 follow), `matz02` to `dng101b2`, its waves (12 kills; flags 114, 116-118), the heal circle
  `matc02` (a hurt player walks back to it; HP is restored), the arena region `matg08` where Blossomon's intro runs (info window, boss HP meter, `sbossbefore`
  demo camera, `BT_SBOSS`), the kill (the floor script sets flag 119), `matz02` to the Terminal (`stagML11` point 0, control returned). All 1,220 sound requests of
  the second floor resolve to prepared clips.
- `test_fieldstage dungeondoors` walks the Humid Cave with **stick input only** (no `MOVETRANS` inside the floors, exits included). A breadth-first search over the live
  walking collision (`dw4_lobby_walk_probe`: the player's own wall/floor solver on an 8-unit grid, so doors, walls and ledges are exactly what the walk meets) gives
  the route; each tick the pad is set to point at the next cell under that tick's camera (`camera_world`, which changes with the box camera), and Cross fights what
  blocks the way. Proven by it: (1) a closed door cannot be passed: the far side is unreachable in the route, and a 120-tick push into the door leaves the player on the
  near side with the solver's wall contact on 105+ of the ticks; (2) when the floor script opens the door (flag set, the named collision mesh removed, wall triangles
  down by 8 per door) the same walk crosses it; (3) `hitland_missing` stays 0 (accessor `dw4_hubruntime_hitland_missing`); (4) the per-tick displacement
  of the walking player stays under 5 units (a door mesh is 10 thick), scripted placements (the boss intro) are counted apart; the doors' hit states are asserted against
  their flags throughout (clear flag = solid). `check-fieldstage` also asserts the closed doors of both floors are in the collision at arrival
  (`doorwall01..03` solid in `dng101b1`; `doorwall01..04`, `06` solid and `05` open in `dng101b2`). `make check-dungeondoors` (under a minute, in `check`) walks to the
  first door and pushes into it; `make check-dungeondoors-full` walks both floors to the Terminal (about three minutes, not in `check`).
  Results of the full walk are in [Stick-only walk: what it reached](#stick-only-walk-what-it-reached).
- **Fixture:** the starter is set to level 40 (the owner-accepted boss-fixture level) with every stat from the recovered recalculation. At level 6 the starter
  does not survive the first Numemon in the port ([#2789](../../docs/findings/findings-2751-3000.md#f2789), unconfirmed: the pilot is a test driver, the arithmetic is from the matched fight code, and no retail
  measurement of a low-level fight exists).

### Stick-only walk: what it reached

`dungeondoors` (full) walks `stag0101`'s hop to `dng101b1` (the one placed hop: the exit region of `stag0101`), then, with the pad alone: `dng101b1` regions `matg01`..`matg07`
(fighting what spawns), the three doors, the exit region `matz02`, `dng101b2` `matg01`..`matg07`, the button, the boss door, Blossomon, the exit door, `matz02`, and the Terminal.
Per floor it asserts the doors above. Observed in the full run: `dng101b1` 3 doors pushed closed and 3 crossed open (`doorwall01` flag 102, `doorwall02`/`03` flag 106);
`dng101b2` doors 2, 3 (button), 4, the boss door 5 and the exit door 6 pushed closed and crossed once open (door 1 had been opened by the wave before the player reached it, so only its
open state and crossing are checked); the button `g_dbutton1` sets flag 128 from one stick-and-Cross swing; the boss door closes at the intro (`ONHIT`: the one scripted placement
puts the player at the arena spot, counted apart from walking) and holds a push, `doorwall06` holds too, and both are open after the kill (flag 119). The walking speed
bound held (largest per-tick step 4.1 units). The run takes about 3 minutes on a desktop CPU (`check-dungeondoors-full`); the quick form
(`check-dungeondoors`, `FIRSTDOOR=1`) walks to `doorwall01` and pushes (under a minute) and is in `check`.

Limits, stated plainly: the route is a test pilot's, not a player model. The hit-land name to mesh order ([#2857](../../docs/findings/findings-2751-3000.md#f2857)) is inferred, not read from the retail receiver. A door is
"closed" if the walking solver's wall set contains its mesh and the flag is clear; the retail door model (`g_d1door` swing animation) is separate and was not compared.
The button was hit by the first swing from 6 units; where retail's button expects the hit from (jump slash, any attack chain step) was not checked.

### Retail checks

**Arrival (savestate in `stag0101`, [#2792](../../docs/findings/findings-2751-3000.md#f2792)).** The `matz00` region of `stag0101` offers the Humid Cave, Cross loads `dng101b1`, and a live read puts the player at
(-300.000, 40.000, 403.792) facing 180; the native floor opens at (-300, 40, 403.825). The retail frame shows the HUD layout the port draws (portrait ring with red/green arcs,
MP dots, LV and number plates, the purple base line). With the player standing and the camera settled, the native frame has the retail composition (region 2 camera at (-300, 130.6, 449.4) looking north and down, a cave wall pair at the bottom with a gap, lit green floor above); only the look was compared, not camera numbers. A lighting/camera mismatch at (-300, 40, 380) ([#2793](../../docs/findings/findings-2751-3000.md#f2793)) is refuted: native frames taken after the test pilot has walked the player to a generator show another corridor under another camera region.

**Sound ([#2795](../../docs/findings/findings-2751-3000.md#f2795)).** A retail memory read of the live `beCri` object on entering `dng101b1` shows `DG_CAVE1` in the BGM handle and `AM_DUNGE` in the ENV handle, the pair the native floor requests (effect handles, volumes and fades are not checked).

**Camera ([#2796](../../docs/findings/findings-2751-3000.md#f2796)).** A retail read of the live camera controller at the `dng101b1` entry corridor holds eye translate (0, 0, 90) and the interest rotation of the Near rig's box 1 (m[0] 0.985, m[2] -0.174, m[8] 0.123), which the native Near rig reproduces (Far would give 98); other boxes and the cut/blend timing are not compared, and the option value was not read in that state, so [#2785](../../docs/findings/findings-2751-3000.md#f2785) stays unconfirmed.

**Boss floor ([#2873](../../docs/findings/findings-2751-3000.md#f2873), [#2874](../../docs/findings/findings-2751-3000.md#f2874)).** On a Hard save (retail `DIFFICULTY` 1, a level-999 fixture from the crown-mod work, stats cross-checked against the ISO table) the `dng101b2` generators read GENE-01/02/06/07 row 27 (HPMAX 637),
GENE-03/04/05 row 17 (624) and the boss GENE-08 row 85 (Blossomon, HPMAX 2640): exactly the rows the port reads from `generatorh.igb` (`check-fieldstage gendrops` with `STAGE=dng101b2 DIFF=1`).
With the emulator paused in the arena after the intro, the sound object's music handle read `BT_SBOSS` (ambience `AM_DUNGE`) and the boss gauge bar spans the top of the frame; the arena has a
treasure chest on its east side. Blind stick pushes and a fixed teleport did not connect (Blossomon keeps moving), but placing the player 5.5 units beside the boss's node, facing it, before each swing did ([#2875](../../docs/findings/findings-2751-3000.md#f2875)):
2640 -> 1898 -> 1187 -> 471 -> 0 in four hits; the boss collapses, a wide demo camera plays, flag 119 is set about ten seconds after the death, and Cross at the arena centre sets `FLAGFOLDER[0]` from 0 to 1
(the ID Key), which is what the port's `loot` recipe asserts. The item's name label only flashes when the player is close. A retail sampling of the boss's sequence name beside the player (`_seqCodeName` of its info object, 160 samples 0.2 s apart) showed only `SQ_WALK` and `SQ_ATCK1` alternating, the port's two dominant sequences

Not checked on retail in the Humid Cave: wave sizes in a live fight, the boss's full attack list and damage ranks, the drop model and pickup effect frames, the chest drops, camera pose numbers beyond the entry corridor, effect audio and timing.

## Difficulty and the generator sets ([#2858](../../docs/findings/findings-2751-3000.md#f2858))

A field world's generators come in three files by difficulty (`separate/generator<n|h|v>.igb`). The hub runtime reads the
common `DIFFICULTY` record (the one `SYSgetDifficulty` reads, 0..2) and opens the matching set. Retail evidence: in the level-999 save used for the retail checks, the common
`DIFFICULTY` record read 1 and all seven `dng101b1` generators latched enemy row 17 (Numemon, HPMAX 624), where the Normal set holds row 13 (HPMAX 54). `check-fieldstage genediff`
asserts rows 13 / 17 for the Normal / Hard sets, that a default save opens the Normal set, and that a save with `DIFFICULTY` 1 opens `generatorh` with HPMAX 624. The route tests keep
the default (Normal) fixture. Not compared: the Very Hard set against retail, the drop lists per tier in a live run, and Blossomon's tier rows (82/85/88 are inferred).

### How the floors open

#### Action2: the blend wait

**The blend wait ([#3050](../../docs/findings/findings-3001-3250.md#f3050)).** Without it `SQ_ATCK3` lasts 94 ticks against retail's 103.0-103.1 (22 samples; [#3012](../../docs/findings/findings-3001-3250.md#f3012)). `beAction2WAITCHECK_Update` case 2 (Motion wait,
`src/unmatched/beAction2WAITCHECK_Update.cpp`) only runs its test when the combiner's `_animationStateList` holds exactly one state. `beAction2MOTIONSET_Start` (`src/unmatched/beAction2MOTIONSET_Start.cpp`)
converts `hokanTime` (frames) to `hokanTicks = round(1e9 * hokanTime / 60)` (1 when it is 0), collapses an unfinished blend (`FUN_001ae780`, only if the list already holds two states) and calls `FUN_001ae660`,
which appends a NEW state (`FUN_001adec0` -> `FUN_001ad990`: combineMode 1, transitionMode 1, status 1, `_baseState` = the state below) with blend start weight 0, delta 1 and duration `hokanTicks`; the old state stays.
The combiner update `FUN_001ac140` (no C source yet, called by Start and every frame by `beAction2MOTIONSET_Update` while the list has more than one state) turns the new state's status to 0 (blending), then
each frame computes ratio = elapsed / `hokanTicks` (clamped to 1) and weight = start + ratio * delta; at weight >= 1.0 (transitionMode 1) it flags the old state status 3 and its final loop removes every status-3
state (`FUN_001ae2d0`). So the list holds two states from the motion's start until `hokanTime` has run out, the Motion wait cannot end before that, and it ends at **max(animation length, blend) after the motion
started**. Mammothmon's `ACTN03`: `actn03` (hokan 8 frames) is 91.02 ticks long, so its wait is unchanged; `actn04` is 3 ticks long with a 12 frame blend, so its wait lasts 12 ticks: 91.02 + 12 = 103.02 ticks, retail
103.0-103.1. A first motion with no state below it (a fresh combiner) is the only state at once and has no blend wait. The host (`src/action2host.c`, `clock_value`) reports a state count of 2 (`MOTWORK+8`) from the motion's
start to start + blend and 1 afterwards; the 1-state wait logic is the recovered one, untouched. The retail state count confirms the model ([#3012](../../docs/findings/findings-3001-3250.md#f3012), [#3050](../../docs/findings/findings-3001-3250.md#f3050); live reads in `dng102b3`): the boss's combiner state count is 2 for ticks 0-8
(actn03's blend), 1 until tick 91.0, 2 from 91.0 to 103.0 (actn04's 12-tick blend) and 1 when `SQ_WAIT` starts, `SQ_ATCK3` 103.1 ticks in 7 of 8 charges (the port runs 103). Not read: the order of the combiner
update against the wait check within one tick (at most one tick), and the intro charge's state count timeline.
`make -C port check-motionset` asserts the 103 for the Mammothmon-shaped program (actn03 hokan 8, actn04 hokan 12), an
unchanged wait when the blend is shorter than the animation, a lone 50 ms motion held 12 ticks by a 12 frame blend after another motion, and the first motion of a fresh combiner not held.

### Pad-only kill of Mammothmon

Findings [#3030](../../docs/findings/findings-3001-3250.md#f3030)-[#3034](../../docs/findings/findings-3001-3250.md#f3034). The port does the same for enemies: a walking enemy is not restored to its old position when no floor is under it (contact bit 2), the knockback hop is not held at the edge, and enemies meet the special wall kinds (8, 0x802) and the dynamic 0x402 props. NPCs, hub actors and the player keep their own solvers.

**What runs ([#3108](../../docs/findings/findings-3001-3250.md#f3108)).** An enemy model's collision query goes out with `DW4_COLLIDE_FALLS` (`modelctrl.h`): a walking enemy uses the player's walking solver (`dw4_lobby_collision_walk_for`: the wall pushes apply, horizontal progress is kept over a drop and bit 2 reports "no floor in the up/down window"), a rising or falling one (vertical channel mode 4 or 1) the airborne solver (`dw4_lobby_collision_air_for`: no floor while rising, the first floor at or below the old height while falling, bit 4 on landing). `model_tick_impl` publishes that as the floor signal (supported = no bit 2 / bit 4, landed = bit 4) and the response of 3926d0 (`dw4_model_floor_response`, the player's own path) runs for the enemy after the waits: floor and fall checks on, not rising, not `SQ_SLEP`, no support -> `SQ_FALL` (`MOVE Fall`, `ACTION WAIT01`, `WCHECK Landing`, `JUMP SQ_WAIT`); a landing clears the vertical channel. The in-tick landing boundary that ends the Landing wait of a hop is kept for enemies, so the knockback timing and the straight 16.35-unit hop are unchanged (`check-gene09refill`: 154-155 ticks). A stage whose collision has no floor triangles has nothing to support or drop a model, so enemies there keep the walls only.

**No floor below ([#3112](../../docs/findings/findings-3001-3250.md#f3112)).** Retail's floor solver (FUN_00388010) casts four segments from y 1000 to -100 and picks the highest triangle inside the height window, else the nearest below the model, else the lowest above it. With no triangle it answers height -1 and all flags 0, and the delivery layer (0x385eec) turns that, and any floor below height 1, into "supported, not landed": the model keeps its height. A walking player or enemy therefore does not fall off an edge with no floor anywhere below it; it walks on in the air (the practice stage past its edge, the Central lips) until it jumps or charge-attacks, then falls with nothing to land on until it drops below y 0, where it lands on the y 0 plane and walks on at the bottom of the map. Only a floor that exists but lies outside the window reports no support and starts `SQ_FALL` (the Goblin Pass ledges). The port follows this (`floor_select` in `lobbycollision.c`, `test_lobbycollision`): an empty window alone does not start `SQ_FALL`, and no enemy is removed for being below the lowest floor.

**`SQ_FALL` in the packs.** All 31 distinct `e_*` enemy models (240 pack infos) have an `SQ_FALL`; none uses `CTRL onFall/noFall` or `MUTEKI OFFFLOOR/OFFWALL`, so the fall check is on in every enemy sequence (`check-fieldstage-enemyfall` asserts the `SQ_FALL` census). The `KIND_ENEMY` objects without one (the `g_*` cannons, turrets and towers, `f_luce2`, `f_devl21..23`) have no recovered fall motion and keep their place; what retail does for them is read from the by-name sequence set only ([#3109](../../docs/findings/findings-3001-3250.md#f3109), unconfirmed): it would store a null program.

**Callbacks live one sequence ([#3110](../../docs/findings/findings-3001-3250.md#f3110)).** The by-name sequence set clears `_luaBHit/_luaWHit/_luaFHit` (asm `0x3981f8..0x398344`); the port clears them in `select_sequence`. ## Loot delivery

Findings [#2794](../../docs/findings/findings-2751-3000.md#f2794) and the rows after it; `src/loot.c`, `include/dw4/loot.h`. A generator drop becomes an item model (`beNDMWMdlItem`). Everything below was read from the sources named; none of it is retail-measured except the x-data amount (finding [#265](../../docs/findings/findings-0251-0500.md#f265)) and the two boss keys' `FLAGFOLDER` writes (the retail checks of each dungeon).

**The model a drop spawns** (`FUN_00384800`, table `0x4c8f30`): category index 0..6 = `conItem`, `insItem`, `impItem`, `weapon`, `armor1`, `armor2`, `abChip`. `impItem` -> `g_000key`, `weapon` -> `g_000weapon`,
both armours -> `g_000armor`, `abChip` -> `g_000access`; `conItem` and `insItem` read the `MODEL` column of their database row (`beDataBase_GetRecordString`, string at `0x50e968`). The real rows: `conItem` 0-9
`g_000item`, 10 `g_000boost1`, 11 `g_000penx`; `insItem` 0 `g_000bit`, 1-3 `g_000xdata1`, 4-25 `g_000xdata2`. The item object is built by `beNDMWMdlItem::vfunc_36` at generation, from the record and the category
(`dbId`), with the LEVEL record of **player 1** (`GAMERAM GETPLAYER1`). `vfunc_36` also looks the model's name up in the table at `0x4cfc10` (the model kind): `g_000bit`, `g_000xdata1`, `g_000xdata2` (kinds 0-2) are
**unnamed**, the other seven (`g_000access`, `g_000armor`, `g_000boost1`, `g_000item`, `g_000key`, `g_000penx`, `g_000weapon`) are **named** (`_isNamed`).

**What triggers a pickup** (`vfunc_38`, `beNDMWMdlPlayer::vfunc_38`): `FUN_003a65f0`/`FUN_003a63b0`/`FUN_003a5cc0` find the nearest player and store the **squared** 3-D distance; the item reacts when it is below 64.0
(8 units, height included; the first player wins a tie). An unnamed model calls the item object's virtual 24 (`beNDMWItemInstant::vfunc_22`) at once: **walking over it is enough**. A named model, while a player is
within the radius and that player's label slot (one drop at a time per player, shared by all item models) is free, shows its name label (`TEXTCTL ITNAMEFONT`) and keeps it while the player stays within the radius.
The player's Cross press (`beNDMWMdlPlayer::vfunc_38`, the `ACTION1` branch) sends `ACTION1` to the `DROPITEM` group; `vfunc_45` answers when its label is the sender's and, if it delivered, appends a true bool, which
makes the player skip the swing; a refused pickup (full) lets the swing go ahead. So weapons, armour, chips, disks and the keys are picked up **with Cross**, BIT / x-data / UPCNT items by **walking over them**.

**Delivery in the port** (`dw4_loot_*`, called from the pickup block of `hubruntime.c` each tick; per drop: the nearest living joined player, labels per player, one Cross press = one pickup):

## Drop models

Findings [#2876](../../docs/findings/findings-2751-3000.md#f2876) to [#2886](../../docs/findings/findings-2751-3000.md#f2886). A drop is a `MODELCTRL` item model (`hubruntime.c`: `loot_drop_hook`, `loot_picked`, `loot_label`, `loot_label_tick`, `loot_model_tick`; the tables in `loot.c`), not only a record.

**Spawn** (`FUN_00384800`, `beNDMWMdlItem::vfunc_36`). The drop hook (run by `fieldgen` for each rolled or injected drop) takes the model name from `dw4_loot_model` (fixed models of the category table at `0x4c8f30`, else the database `MODEL` column), finds its first free instance with
`dw4_scriptservices_first_inactive` and sends `MODELCTRL <model> <slot> GENERATE V<position>`; the item roll (`dw4_loot_make`) follows, so the draws keep their order. Retail's GENERATE also carries the record and the category index (`AppendInt` twice); the port keeps them in the drop record.
The first player's `LEVEL` feeds the roll. `dw4_drop.model`, `.model_slot` and `.model_live` name the instance; `dw4_hubruntime_loot_stats` counts the rest.

| Case | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| model has no controller in the stage | the drop stays record-only and `unmodelled` counts it (`missing` names it). None occurs: all ten `g_000*` models (`bit`, `xdata1`, `xdata2`, `access`, `armor`, `boost1`, `item`, `key`, `penx`, `weapon`) exist in every `dng*` pack, `stag0000`, `stag0001`, `stag0101` and (by the hub runtime) `stagGF01..03`; only the hub and interface stages (`stagml*`, `stagis*`, `stag0103`, `stag0203`) carry none, and they never drop (`check-fieldstage lootmodels` prints the pools: eight instances each, `g_000key` one) |
| every instance busy | `SelectReceivers(..., -2)` finds no receiver in retail, so no item object exists: the drop is lost (`lost`), nothing is rolled (the world stream is untouched; the test asserts it) |
| uncollected | the authored program runs `SQ_ENTR`/`SQ_ENT2`, then `WAIT01` with a 960-frame wait, then `SQ_BODING` (blink), `SQ_EXIT` and `SQ_ERAS`: the model blinks for about 110 frames and ends about 1145 frames after the drop, the item with it (`expired`; the test measures 1145 frames: `SQ_BODING` from frame 1020 to 1133 of the measurement, then `SQ_EXIT`). `g_000key` has no `SQ_BODING` and stays. Not measured in retail |
| picked up | the model is sent `SQ_DDMG` (its `DEAD01` fade, then `SQ_ERAS`): the unnamed models' key event after delivery, the named models' answer to `DROPITEM` (`vfunc_45`); `erased` counts it and the instance is free again |

**The blink-out** (`FLASH1`, [#2910](../../docs/findings/findings-2751-3000.md#f2910) to [#2912](../../docs/findings/findings-2751-3000.md#f2912)). A scan of every `beAction2Info` action in the archives (all `beModelCtrlInfo` records of all packs, 109216 actions) finds at most 24 components: 922 actions have 23 (the item and pickup `FLASH1`), 84 have 24, two 22, one 18,
the rest 14 or fewer. A host of sixteen components cannot run the item models' `FLASH1`; the Action2 host holds
`DW4_A2_MAX_COMPONENTS` = 32: the first 16 component records keep their addresses in the oracle image (`DW4_A2_IMAGE_COMPONENTS`, so the title oracle's snapshot is unchanged), the others are mapped objects after the image (`COMPX`), the list entry array has room for 32 and the list capacity word
is 16 until a longer program is started. `dw4_modelctrl_long_actions` counts an action longer than 32 and the test asserts it stays zero.
The authored `FLASH1` is a `JOINTSET` 200..320 (the item spins, about 120 frames) and ten pairs `WAITCHECK Frame 10` / `FADESET OUT 10 1`: each `OUT` starts a fresh 10-frame fade from 1 to 0, so the item shows ten sawtooth blinks. A fade starts
when the host runs the component (`fade_event` of the host, applied by `modelctrl.c` after the start and after every host tick), which is the retail order, so the ten restarts happen. Every action with a `FADESET` behind a wait (the item `DEAD01`: `Frame 4`, then `OUT 7 1`) fades when its wait ends, not at its start. The floating damage numbers are such programs (`i_dmgnum` `OPEN01`: `FADESET IN 12`, wait 20, `OUT 20`, wait 20): they fade in over 12 frames and out from frame 32 (the `attack` test checks the digit colour at tick 12; the retail look of the numbers over time is not compared).
The lobby draws a world model blended when its Action2 fade is below 1 (`lobby.c`).
`check-fieldstage loot` asserts (eight uncollected `g_000bit` models): `SQ_BODING` starts at least 900 frames in and lasts over 100 frames, the model's alpha is below 0.5 in at least 20 frames and above 0.9 in at least 20, the fade restarts at least eight times, the lowest alpha is below 0.2, the models end (frames 1000 to 1200), and no model of the pools skipped an action
(`dw4_modelctrl_long_actions` is zero). `DW4_LOOT_BLINK=<prefix>` writes eight frames of six uncollected drops in `SQ_BODING` 14 frames apart (`<prefix>-blink0..7.png`; not committed). In them the blue disk's card is solid, spinning and fading to nearly clear: alpha 0.8, 0.5, 0.2 then the restart at 1.0. Items outside the lit part of this floor draw dark on dark.

**Cross press and the swing** ([#2913](../../docs/findings/findings-2751-3000.md#f2913), [#2914](../../docs/findings/findings-2751-3000.md#f2914)). `beNDMWMdlPlayer::vfunc_38`'s Cross branch (not in the main lobby) sends `ACTION1` to the `DROPITEM` group with the player's id, and swings (`SET_KEYNAME ...
